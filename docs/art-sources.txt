Text-art sources

Ship, invaders, and explosion shapes adapted from Alternate Space Invaders
by WaffleKilometer, released under CC0 (public domain).

https://opengameart.org/content/alternate-space-invaders
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Conversion: each pair of source pixel rows becomes a row of Unicode half-block
characters (space, ▀, ▄, █). Two original poses provide the alien animation.
The playable board uses text; it does not load the source sprite image.

Borders and controls use standard Unicode box-drawing characters.
